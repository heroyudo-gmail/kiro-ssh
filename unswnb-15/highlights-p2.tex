\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item We study cross-network shift and adversarial evasion on one lightweight NIDS.
  \item Few-shot recovers cross-network generalization; adversarial training does not.
  \item The generalization-robustness interaction depends on direction AND attack strength.
  \item A protocol-consistent feature-space (PCFS) attack avoids over-claiming evasion.
  \item Three-stage AWS validation: pipeline transfers for generalization, not evasion.
\end{itemize}
